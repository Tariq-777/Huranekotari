The human behind the handle

Born in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia to a Filipina mother and an Egyptian father — so I'm a bit of everything. Right now I'm based in Egypt, studying Computer Science.
English is my native language, followed closely by Arabic. I understand some Tagalog thanks to my mother's side, and have a basic grasp of French and Spanish.

English Arabic Tagalog French Spanish
Web Design came through university — studying CS introduced me to it, even if I wasn't immediately in love with it. It grew on me.

Voice Acting was a natural extension of my love for theatre and drama. Performance was always something I was drawn to, and VA became the outlet for that.

Events happened through my university club, LGSY.gg — and it happened fast. I hold the record for the fastest back-to-back promotions: Member → Co-Head HR → Head HR, all in a single semester. We organize events inside and outside the university, with the biggest being Egycon, the largest anime convention in Egypt.
I love all forms of art — drawing, singing, and playing instruments are all things I dabble in. On the sports side, I play football, basketball, and volleyball. I wouldn't call myself a genius at any of it, but I pick things up fast and enjoy the process.
